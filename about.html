Our Story

From a side project to a full creative studio.

Lumen started as a two-person freelance team working out of a tiny apartment. We loved design, we loved building things, and we wanted to work with clients who cared about the details.

Eight years later, we're a full creative studio with designers, developers and strategists — but our values haven't changed. We still treat every project like it's our own, and we still obsess over the small things.

  • User-first design thinking
  • Honest, transparent communication
  • Long-term partnerships, not one-off gigs
  • Quality over shortcuts — always
